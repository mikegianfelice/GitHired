Service

GitHired provides a Canada-first view of technology jobs discovered from public employer ATS boards, plus optional agent access via MCP. The service is provided free of charge, as-is, without warranties of completeness or fitness for a particular purpose.

Acceptable use

  • Do not attempt to bypass rate limits, disrupt the service, or scrape in ways that overload infrastructure.
  • Do not use GitHired to auto-submit job applications or misrepresent eligibility.
  • Do not present model-invented salaries or Canadian eligibility as GitHired data.
  • Respect employer application processes and third-party site terms when you apply.

Data accuracy

Listings can change or close without notice. Salaries shown are employer-advertised figures only. Geographic eligibility is deterministic and may exclude ambiguous cases. GitHired is not a complete labour-market census.
